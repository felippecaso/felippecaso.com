I'm a Lead Engineer at [Monzo](https://monzo.com), working in applied AI in the Platform team, building products that make our data team better, from AI coding assistants to the agents we run on our data.

I've been coding since 2008 and working in data teams since my second year of university, mostly at startups and scale-ups before Monzo. Since 2016 I've also taught and mentored people in data, programming and career development, including speaking at conferences (most recently AI Engineer New York), all of which are among my favourite parts of the job.

My journey was unusual: I graduated in Electrical Engineering from one of the top 10 federal universities in Brazil, with an exchange year at Queen Mary University of London studying Audio Systems Engineering. That's where my artistic side (I'm a musician!) met the exact sciences, and the mix still shapes how I work, being rigorous about the data and human about the people using it.
